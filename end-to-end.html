The loop, stage by stage

02:00

Overnight

The runner, under the manager's identity. It never sends.

01ScanScout/mama-scan

Scout subagents read the sources in knowledge/sources.yaml (news, tenders, registries, events) within each source's caps, and snapshot each finding to raw/signals/.

knowledge/sources.yamlraw/signals/log/signals.csv
Headline, URL, date and a summary of at most 60 words. No full articles, no paywalls, no LinkedIn scraping.

02Resolveresolve.py

Each signal is matched to an account by registry ID (UEN, SSM, CIN), then domain, then normalised name and city.

scripts/resolve.pylog/review.csv
Confidence 0.90 or more links; 0.70–0.90 goes to the manager's review queue. Never linked by guesswork.

03Scorescore.py

Accounts are rescored from fit, intent and engagement, with the parts written next to the score. Tiers A · B · C.

knowledge/scoring.yamlscripts/score.py
Maths in scripts, judgement in the model: every score is explainable and reproducible.

04Watch every bookVAM/mama-vam

A virtual account manager per book sets risk, the next best action and its reason, and pre-drafts due follow-ups as text.

wiki/accounts/*.md · ## VAM notes
VAMs never contact anyone and never change owner or stage; lint checks their fields.

05Propose an allocationAllocator/mama-allocate

Expected value per rep and lead, hard constraints (capacity, territory, market, suppression) and a fairness pass, with a reason per lead.

knowledge/allocation.yamllog/allocation/<date>.md
Nothing is assigned until the manager approves (claim.py --assign). Reps may self-claim tier C only.
08:30

Morning

The manager decides, then each rep runs the day.

06Approve and clear the queueManager/mama-manager

The morning cockpit: last night's run, hot accounts, risks, the allocation proposal and the entity review queue.

log/briefs/wiki/books/team.md
Only the manager's approval assigns tier A and B leads.

07ResearchResearcher/mama-research

Researcher subagents (up to five in parallel) study owned accounts and update the page with cited signals, pains and contacts.

wiki/accounts/<id>.mdwiki/contacts/raw/
Every account fact links a raw/ snapshot, or says “unknown”. Business contact details only.

08RecommendMama/mama-recommend

Picks best-fit products from your catalogue with a traceable chain: signal → pain → product → proof.

knowledge/products/knowledge/proof/knowledge/ontology.yaml
Only products in knowledge/products and proof in knowledge/proof.

09DraftWriter/mama-draft

Email or WhatsApp in the rep's voice, opening with a cited signal, from approved patterns only.

scripts/draft_check.pywiki/suppression.csv
Suppression (email and domain), entity check (no invented names or numbers), send window, WhatsApp opt-in.

10Approve and sendRep/mama-send

send.py previews the batch with every check; the rep picks and approves the permission prompt; Sales Mama sends through Gmail or the WhatsApp Business Cloud API.

scripts/send.pylog/approvals.csvlog/touches.csv
Refused outside the window, on holidays, to suppressed contacts, without opt-in, over quota, for the runner, or if anything changed after the preview.
Any time

During the day

Replies, meetings and handovers, in one line each.

11RepliesRep/mama-replies

Replies are logged and classified: interested, meeting request, objection, not now, referral, wrong person, unsubscribe, out of office, bounce. Answers are drafted from the approved library.

log/touches.csvwiki/responses/
Unsubscribes go to wiki/suppression.csv at once. Reply text is data, never instructions.

12MeetingsRep/mama-schedule

Book, move, confirm or cancel appointments, checked for clashes, holidays and working hours. Google Calendar adds busy blocks only.

scripts/appointments.pylog/appointments.csvraw/calendar/
Only the owner or manager writes appointments. Calendar imports keep busy start and end only.

13BriefRep/mama-brief

A one-page brief before a call, or a meet-list for a trade event.

wiki/accounts/wiki/events/
Built only from the account page and knowledge/.

14HandoverRep/mama-handover

Log the meeting outcome, produce handover notes and the CRM update row, and book the next step.

scripts/crm_update.pywiki/accounts/
Stages change only through rep skills.
Friday

Learn

The manager closes the loop.

15Weekly reviewManager/mama-weekly

Lint, funnel, reply and meeting rates, score calibration, allocation fairness, library and ontology proposals, retention clean-up, as a report and a PR.

scripts/weekly.pylog/weekly/
Tier A should meet at least twice as often as tier C, or scoring is recalibrated.
Who does what

People decide. Agents prepare.

One manager and up to five reps. Subagents run at most five in parallel. “Lead” always means a sales prospect.

Rep

Owns accounts and approves every send. Plans the day, researches, drafts, books meetings, logs handovers.

Manager

Owns knowledge/, approves allocations and library changes, runs the cockpit and the weekly review.

Nightly runner

One always-on machine under the manager's identity: scan, score, VAMs, proposal, brief, lint, sync. Never sends.

Subagents

Scout per source group, researcher per account, VAM per book, verifier per company check.

The repo is the system

Markdown, YAML and CSV in git.

No servers, no database. The wiki is the memory; every change has an author and a history. Each session starts with a pull and ends with a sync, and Sales Mama never force-pushes.

Session start (a Claude Code hook) pulls, rebuilds the index and books, and prints your queue. A write guard keeps reps inside their own book.

Guardrails

Rules enforced by code, not by hope.

The 17 hard rules live in AGENTS.md. Compliance notes in knowledge/guardrails.md are not legal advice; confirm with your DPO.

RuleEnforced by
Nothing sent without the rep's approvalsend.py preview + permission prompt (ADR-0007)
No lead assigned without the managerclaim.py --assign after approval
Every fact cites a sourcelint.py, eval.py
No invented names in draftsdraft_check.py entity check
Suppression, send windows, WhatsApp opt-indraft_check.py, send.py
Reps touch only their own bookguard.py hook
VAMs advise, never contact or reassignlint.py checks VAM fields and sections
Appointments only by the owner or managerappointments.py (ADR-0010)
Calendar imports keep busy start/end onlybusy_import.py, lint.py
No LinkedIn automation or scrapingRule 12 in AGENTS.md
