01 knowledge/company.md

Who you are and how you sound

Words
It controls
What you sell, the value you bring, and your voice. Every draft and brief starts here.
Change it to
Describe your business in plain English, as you would to a new hire.
knowledge/company.md · B2C
# Company
## What we sell
Lumen Pilates runs two studios in Singapore (Tampines, Bishan) and one in Kuala Lumpur.
We sell class packs, monthly memberships and a first-visit intro offer to adults who
want a stronger back and better posture. Our customers are people who booked a class,
asked about one, or bought from us before.

## Value props
- Never run out mid-month: we remind you before your last class (`routine`).
- Back after a break: a gentle restart plan with your usual instructor (`restart`).

## Voice
Friendly, encouraging, short. First names. No pressure and no exclamation marks.
knowledge/company.md · fundraising
# Company
## What we do
Harbour Hope is a registered charity in Singapore with a partner centre in Chennai.
We run free after-school learning for children from low-income families. We raise from
companies (CSR and employee volunteering), foundations and grant-makers, and from
monthly donors who chose to hear from us.

## Value props
- See where your CSR budget goes: a named centre and a report every term (`csr_budget`).
- Engage your people: volunteering days with our learners (`employee_engagement`).

## Voice
Warm and specific, never guilt. One story, one number, one ask.

03 knowledge/ontology.yaml

The words Sales Mama may use

Lists
It controls
The closed vocabulary: segments, the signals that mean “now is a good time”, the needs they imply and the people who feel them. Accounts can only be tagged with these terms.
Change it to
Your own moments that matter: an enquiry, a pack running low, a CSR report, a grant call.
Change the ontology before you import customers or donors: lint.py flags any page tagged with a term that is no longer here.
knowledge/ontology.yaml · B2C
industries: [returning_member, new_enquiry, corporate_wellness]
signals:
  enquiry_received:  {implies: [getting_started], weight: 1.0, decay_days: 14}
  trial_booked:      {implies: [getting_started], weight: 0.9, decay_days: 21}
  pack_running_low:  {implies: [routine],         weight: 0.9, decay_days: 30}
  membership_ending: {implies: [renewal_window],  weight: 1.0, decay_days: 45}
  lapsed_60_days:    {implies: [restart],         weight: 0.7, decay_days: 90}
  new_year:          {implies: [], weight: 0.4, decay_days: 21, hook: true}   # season, not a person
  moved_away:        {negative: true, weight: -0.8, decay_days: 365}
pains:
  getting_started: {personas: [member]}
  routine:         {personas: [member]}
  renewal_window:  {personas: [member]}
  restart:         {personas: [member]}
personas:
  member: {title_patterns: []}

07 knowledge/scoring.yaml

What matters most

Lists
It controls
How fit, intent, past behaviour and engagement add up to a score, and the tier cut-offs.
Change it to
B2C: lean on intent and engagement; people have no head count. Fundraising: give past giving real weight.
Keep the other keys from the shipped file; change only the numbers you mean to.
knowledge/scoring.yaml · B2C
weights: {fit: 0.15, intent: 0.55, propensity: 0.0, engagement: 0.30}
fit: {industry_match: 0.5, size_in_range: 0.0, region_match: 0.3,
      owns_prerequisite: 0.2, unknown: 0.5}     # size off: people have no head count
tiers: {A: 0.65, B: 0.40}
knowledge/scoring.yaml · fundraising

propensity: {missing: 0.0}      # past giving, 0–1, from your donor export
tiers: {A: 0.70, B: 0.45}
08 knowledge/playbook.md

How you follow up

Words
It controls
Sequences, timing and message rules every draft follows.
Change it to
B2C: short, quick, one reminder at most. Fundraising: thank first, report, then ask.
knowledge/playbook.md · B2C
## Sequences
- New enquiry: reply within one working day (WhatsApp if they opted in, else email) → fu1 after 2 days → stop.
- Pack running low: one note when 2 classes are left. Never more than one reminder a week.
- Lapsed 60 days: one “we kept your spot” message, then nothing.

## Message rules
- At most 60 words on WhatsApp, 90 by email. First name, one offer, one question.
knowledge/playbook.md · fundraising
## Sequences
- Company: first email cites their CSR focus → fu1 after 5 working days → a call.
- New gift: thank within 48 hours → impact update at 30 days → renewal talk 60 days before the pledge ends.
- Never ask in the same message you thank.

## Message rules
- One story, one number from knowledge/proof/, one ask. At most 120 words.

8. Personal data: only people who gave us their details (an enquiry, a booking,

Rules to agree first

Customers and donors are people.

Shipped Sales Mama handles business contacts only. Before real customer or donor data goes in, agree these with your DPO. Not legal advice.

Consent and purpose

Only people who gave you their details and agreed to hear from you. Keep only what the purpose needs; delete on request.

Calls and SMS

In Singapore, check the DNC Registry before calling or texting a number. Sales Mama never automates calls or SMS.

WhatsApp

Recorded opt-in, an approved template for first contact, an opt-out line, inside the send window.

Tax receipts (SG)

Only gifts to an approved IPC are tax-deductible. Never promise a deduction unless you hold IPC status (Charity Portal).

Public appeals (SG)

Charities and permit holders submit details of public fund-raising appeals on the Charity Portal before the appeal starts.

India

Companies' CSR money can go only to registered implementing agencies (Form CSR-1); foreign contributions to Indian NGOs need FCRA registration.

Questions

Before you start.

Do I need to write code?
No. For B2C and fundraising you edit text and short YAML lists in knowledge/, plus your rules with your DPO. Claude Code can do the edits for you.
Can one team do B2B and B2C?
Yes: put both kinds of segment in ontology.yaml and limit each offer with fits.industries. For two very different businesses, one repo each is easier to keep clean.
Where do B2C customers come from?
From your own lists only: bookings, purchases, enquiries and sign-ups, imported with /mama-ingest. The web tells Sales Mama when (seasons, school terms, local events), never who.
Can I add a country beyond Singapore, Malaysia and India?
Not by configuration: the scripts know three markets. Adding one is engine work in scripts/.
Will updates to Sales Mama overwrite my changes?
Your business lives in knowledge/, reps/ and your rules. Engine updates touch scripts/ and skills, so pulling them rarely conflicts; git shows you anything that does.
Can I rename “account” and “lead” in the skills?
You don't need to: Claude reads your company.md and speaks your language. If you want to, edit .agents/skills/ and run python3 scripts/sync_skills.py.
